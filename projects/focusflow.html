What is Focusflow

FocusFlow is a calming productivity app that helps college students stay focused during solo study sessions. It helps users track focus time, reflect on their progress, and build better study habits without the pressure of traditional productivity tools.

For this personal case study, I researched student study habits and designed the full experience from early research to final prototype. The final design was tested with peers, who described the app as friendly, simple, and useful for staying on track.

Problem

Students need more than saved listings

Students struggle to stay focused when studying because they can’t track how they spend their time. Without tools that provide feedback or structure, many students get distracted and lose momentum, which leads to frustration and burnout.

Reflection

What did I learn?

As my first case study, FocusFlow taught me how to make design decisions with more intention, especially under a short timeline. Since the full project was completed in only two weeks, I couldn’t explore every idea in depth, so I focused on what mattered most: creating a focus tool that felt calm, simple, and supportive instead of stressful.

This project also helped me understand the trade-off between keeping an experience clean and adding more features. Even though testers responded positively to the welcoming interface and easy flow, I know the project would be stronger with more diverse testing, more time spent on mid-fidelity iterations, and thoughtful customization options like themes or gentle notifications. Moving forward, I want to keep building on this low-pressure experience while making the app feel more personal to different student routines.
